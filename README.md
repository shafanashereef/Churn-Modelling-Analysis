# 📊 Churn Modelling Analysis


## 📝 Overview
This project analyzes customer churn to identify the factors that influence whether customers stay or leave a company.  
The notebook includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualizations, and interpretation of customer behavior patterns.

---

## 📁 Dataset Information
The dataset contains customer demographic, financial, and activity-related information.  
Key columns include:

- **CustomerID** – Unique identifier  
- **Geography** – Country or region  
- **Gender**  
- **Age**  
- **Tenure** – Years with the company  
- **Balance** – Account balance  
- **NumOfProducts** – Number of subscribed products  
- **HasCrCard** – Credit card ownership  
- **IsActiveMember** – Active customer status  
- **EstimatedSalary**  
- **Exited** – Target variable (1 = churned, 0 = stayed)

---

## ⭐ Key Features of the Dataset
- Clearly labeled numeric and categorical features  
- Suitable for churn prediction modeling  
- Balanced combination of financial and behavioral attributes  
- Contains the target variable needed for classification tasks  

---

## 🛠 Technologies Used

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis & Insights

### 1️⃣ Demographic Insights
- Customers aged **40–50** show the highest churn probability.  
- Certain regions have significantly higher churn rates.

### 2️⃣ Engagement Insights
- **Inactive members** churn far more often than active ones.  
- Customers with **only 1 product** have greater churn likelihood.

### 3️⃣ Financial Behavior
- High account balances may correlate with dissatisfaction-driven churn.  
- Credit card ownership alone is not a strong churn predictor.

### 4️⃣ Tenure & Loyalty
- Mid-tenure customers (not new, not long-term) are more likely to churn.  
- Longer-tenure customers are more loyal.

---

## ✔️ Recommendations
- Increase **customer engagement** for inactive members (rewards, follow-ups).  
- Create **targeted retention campaigns** for high-risk age groups.  
- Investigate **service issues** in regions with above-average churn.  
- Promote **multi-product bundles** to increase customer stickiness.  
- Implement **churn prediction models** for proactive customer retention.

---

## ✅ Conclusion
The analysis highlights key demographic, financial, and behavioral factors driving customer churn.  
These insights support business decision-making by helping identify at-risk customers and improving retention strategies.  
Future work can include advanced modeling, segmentation, or real-time churn monitoring.
